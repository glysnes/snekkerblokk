How to use Snekkerblokk

Pencil mode: the Pencil draws, your finger moves things. It turns on by itself the first time you use Apple Pencil, and can be turned off under Settings. Your finger drags points, dimensions and the handle on arcs in every tool. On empty paper it moves the sheet, and two fingers zoom. A tap selects. Whole lines and circles are moved with Move.

Shapes: Rectangle, Circle and Arc are drawn by dragging with the Pencil. An arc is dragged from end to end and gets a standard curve. Drag the handle in the middle of the arc with Move to adjust it by eye.

Guide lines are dashed and are left out of the parts list. Use them to anchor things, for example the centre of a circle. The Pencil snaps to the middle of a line when you see “mid”. Turn a regular line into a guide line in the panel, or press X.

Trim: tap the piece that sticks out, and it is trimmed at the nearest crossing with a line, circle or arc. The line gets new end points and correct dimensions. A circle becomes an arc. You can drag the Pencil across several pieces.

Tape: tap a line to see how long each piece is between crossings, and the angles where it crosses other lines. Tap a second object (point, line or circle) to measure the distance between them, and lock it as a constraint if you like. In the panel you can pin the dimensions to the drawing, so they stay and are printed, or split the line at the crossings.

Select: drag a box around objects, or tap them to add or remove. In the panel you can copy, paste, duplicate, delete, mirror across a line or flip. With Move you drag the whole selection. Keyboard: ⌘C, ⌘V, ⌘D, Delete.

Dimension in the way? Drag the dimension or angle wherever you want it, with Pencil or finger. Drag it back and it returns to its place. A short tap lets you type a new value.

Change a dimension: tap the number in the drawing and type the value you want. The dimension is locked and the line changes. Locked dimensions have a coloured background.

Locked or free: lines in the same colour as the text (black, or white in dark mode) are locked by dimensions and constraints. Blue lines can still move, as in Fusion.

Constraints decide how things relate: horizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular, equal length, midpoint. Select a line or a point and tap a constraint. Constraints hold while you drag with Move.

Lengths are measured point to point along the line. Allow for material thickness yourself. Saw angles are measured from a square cut (0°).

Print on A4

On iPad: tap Save PDF and choose Print or Save to Files.
